Design Tokens

The small rules that make everything feel consistent.

Tokens are the reusable values behind the website: fonts, sizes, spacing, radius, shadows, widths, borders, and motion. Future versions will make most of these editable.

Typography

Use a restrained editorial scale.

The homepage gets the largest voice. Internal pages and supporting sections step down so every component does not feel like a hero.

Manrope for confident headings.
Source Sans 3 for readable body copy and documentation.
Georgia for rare editorial moments.
