Decision Journal

We remember why the system works.

The decision journal prevents future teams from undoing thoughtful choices because they only see the final design and not the reasoning behind it.

Why pricing is visible early

Trust matters more than collecting unqualified demo calls. Rally should never make a visitor feel tricked by setup fees, monthly plans, or support costs.

Why the homepage hero uses full imagery

The homepage needs an emotional arrival moment. Internal pages can be quieter because the visitor already understands where they are.

Why photography comes before software

Rally serves organizers and communities. Software is the support system, not the hero of the story.

Why components need text links

Small blurbs feel unfinished without a next step. Subtle links make them feel intentional without adding heavy CTAs.

v3 decisions

Decisions added after Website v15.

D-021

Studio is the single source of truth.

Brand guidelines are part of Foundations rather than a separate document.

D-022

Resources and Support have separate jobs.

Resources develops organizers; Support helps people use Rally. Product updates belong in Support as “What’s New in Rally.”

D-023

One system can support distinct add-ons.

Add-ons may have focused identities while sharing interaction behavior, components, tokens, and accessibility rules.

D-024

Website v15 is the production benchmark.

Its frozen colors, type, spacing, radius, shadows, component treatment, and page patterns replace earlier exploratory values.

Rally Studio Phase 1 establishes the application shell, shared tokens, navigation, search, and live token engine. Future phases expand the component library and export-ready WordPress patterns.
Rally Studio v3.0 is the shared brand, design, and experience system for the Rally website, product, documentation, and future add-ons.
